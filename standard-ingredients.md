# Standard Ingredients

When a recipe or meal mentions a **generic name** (left column), the household actually uses the
**specific product** (right column). Use the specific product for Cronometer logging, custom-food
creation, and recipe builds — search Cronometer for the exact product first, and only fall back to a
generic entry if the branded item isn't in the database.

Applies to both Cronometer accounts (Harsh = account A, Vruddhi = account B) unless noted.

| Generic name | Standard product we use | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Monk fruit / monk fruit sweetener | Whole Earth Monk Fruit Allulose Blend | Allulose-based blend, not pure monk fruit — carbs differ from pure extract |
| Greek yogurt | Kirkland Organic Non-Fat Greek Yogurt | Plain, non-fat |
| Almond milk | Califia Farms Unsweetened Organic Almond Milk | Unsweetened |
| 2% milk | a2 Milk (Reduced Fat 2%) | a2 protein variety |
| Nut butter | NuttZo Organic 7 Nut & Seed Butter | Per Harsh 2026-09-03 |
| Ghee | Homemade ghee, made from Kerrygold Unsalted Pure Irish Butter | Home-rendered; nutrition ≈ generic ghee / clarified butter (~112 kcal/tbsp, pure fat). Log as ghee, not butter |
| Tofu / firm tofu | Nasoya Organic Super Firm Tofu | Super-firm (denser, higher protein than firm/extra-firm) |
| Ajwain / carom seeds | Pride of India Ajwain Seed (Whole, Organic) | Cronometer food id 25866761 ("Pride Of India, Ajwain Seed Whole Organic Spices"). NOT mustard seed — Cronometer's fuzzy import matched "ajwain" to Mustard Seed (id 450516) once; always pick 25866761 |
| Fat-free / skim milk | Nature's Promise Organic Fat Free Milk | **Log as USDA "Milk, Nonfat, Fluid, with Added Vitamin A and Vitamin D" — Cronometer food id 168** (245 g/cup, 34 kcal/100 g). Do NOT use the branded entry id 75460528: its "Cup" measure is 100 g and its per-100 g values are per-cup label values, so 120 g counts as 1.2 cups instead of 0.5 cup (per Harsh, 2026-09-04) |

## How to extend this list

Add a new row whenever a generic ingredient gets a settled standard product. Keep one row per
generic term. If Harsh and Vruddhi use different brands for the same item, split into two rows and
note the account in the Notes column.
